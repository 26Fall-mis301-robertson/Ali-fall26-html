Your next side quest starts with coffee.

Welcome to Side Quest Coffee Co., a neighborhood coffee shop in Juniper Falls where a quick coffee can turn into an afternoon around the game table.

Settle in with something to eat, share a physical board or card game, or stop by for a meeting. Whether you play every week or have no idea where to start, there is a place for you here.
